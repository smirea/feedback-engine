The Share menu ends with **New Webhook**, which adds a webhook from the editor: a name, a URL, an optional header, and whether to attach the JSON export and the image export, as PNG or JPEG with a compression. The browser keeps it for the site, and choosing it opens it to send, change, or delete it. Its post carries `title`, `image`, `feedback`, and `device`. Webhooks the app lists keep their request.
