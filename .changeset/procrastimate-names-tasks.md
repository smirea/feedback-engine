A ProcrastiMate post now carries a `titleSuggestion` with the feedback items, and ProcrastiMate's server renames an untouched `<screen> Feedback: N changes` task to a short summary, such as `3 Groceries Feedback: bigger titles, remove the share button`, a second after it is created. Posting never waits for it. `taskTitleInstructions` and `summarized()` in `@stefan.inc/feedback-engine/core` are deprecated and go in the next major version.
